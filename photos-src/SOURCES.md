# Photo sources and licenses

All ten source photos were downloaded on 2026-11-17 from the listed pages. On that date each Unsplash page showed **"Free to use under the Unsplash License"**, and the Pexels page was a "Free Stock Photo" under the Pexels License. Neither license requires attribution, but credit is given here anyway.

Neither license grants model or property releases. People and the identities shown in the photos must not be presented as endorsing a real business. In this demo, the coach names are illustrative and are attached to stock portraits. **Before using this design for a real studio, replace the coach portraits with photos of the actual coaches.**

## Notes from the photo review

- Crops were chosen to keep apparel and gym branding out of frame. The functional crop excludes a participant wearing a printed tank top.
- A few small marks are still visible at full resolution: a tiny chest mark on a T-shirt outside the HIIT crop, and small text on the mats and rack hooks. None of them are readable at the sizes the site uses.
- `npm run images` applies one shared grade to every photo (slightly muted colour and a little more contrast) and prints the coaches in monochrome, so the photos read as one series.
